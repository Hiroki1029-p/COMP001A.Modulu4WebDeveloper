Our Story

Ichiraku is a traditional ramen shop that has been serving delicious ramen for over 20 years. Our secret recipes and fresh ingredients make us a favorite among locals and tourists alike.
At Ichiraku, we offer a variety of ramen made with rich, flavorful broth and fresh noodles. Our menu includes classic options such as Shoyu Ramen, Miso Ramen, and Tonkotsu Ramen. We also serve delicious side dishes, including gyoza and fried rice.
Our friendly staff and warm atmosphere make Ichiraku a great place to enjoy a relaxing meal with family and friends. Whether you are visiting Japan for the first time or are a regular customer, we hope you enjoy the authentic taste of Ichiraku.
